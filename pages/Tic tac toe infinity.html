Tic tac toe infinity is a special version of Tic tac toe. The rules are as follows:
• 3 symbols horizontally, vertically, diagonally = 1 point
• The one with the most points wins
• If the number of points is the same, the one who got the point first wins
• If the number of points is zero, there will be a draw
• Have fun
